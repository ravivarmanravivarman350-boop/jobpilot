# JobPilot - AI-Powered Job Search & Application Agent for Tamil Nadu Freshers

## Mission
"Find the right job. Verify it. Prepare for it. Apply with confidence."

## Target Users
- College students (Final year & pre-final year)
- Fresh graduates (2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 batches)
- First-time job seekers
- Internship seekers
- Tamil Nadu-based freshers (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem, Hosur, etc.)

## Main Features
1. **AI Job Search**: Smart matching for entry-level roles, off-campus drives, & internships with match scores and TN salary benchmarks.
2. **Fake Job Detector**: Live scam verification system analyzing email domains, fee demands, salary patterns, & employer signals.
3. **Skill Gap Analysis**: Interactive skill matching against target roles with a 4-week personalized fresher roadmap.
4. **AI Resume & Cover Letter Studio**: ATS-friendly resume builder & automated customized cover letters for tech & core roles.
5. **AI Mock Interview Simulator**: TCS, Zoho, Freshworks & TN Off-Campus Drive interview questions with instant AI scoring & answers.
6. **Application Tracker**: Full application management lifecycle with deadline alerts and status pipeline.
